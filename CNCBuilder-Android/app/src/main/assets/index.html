A 1 : 16 taper changes the diameter by 0.0625 mm per mm of length (¾ inch per foot). Half angle 1° 47′ 24″. On the lathe the taper goes in G76 / G92 as R = radius difference from start to end point; in CYCLE97 as two diameters DM1 and DM2.

Tool nose radius compensationLathe · G41 / G42 / G40

  • G42 for OD turning toward the chuck, G41 for boring. G40 cancels.
  • Put the nose radius in the R column and the tip number in the T column of the geometry or wear offset.
  • Switch it on and off on a G00 or G01 move that is longer than the nose radius, never on G02 / G03.
  • Without it, every taper, chamfer and radius is cut with the nose radius error. Straight Z and X cuts are not affected.
  • Sinumerik stores the tip as cutting edge position in the tool data (same 1 – 9 numbering) and uses the same G41 / G42 / G40.

Diagram drawn with +X up and +Z to the right, as seen on a rear (slant-bed) turret. Confirm the numbering for your machine in its offset manual.

Cutter radius compensationMilling · G41 / G42 / G40

  • Program the part outline. The control offsets the tool by the radius in the D register.
  • G41 with M03 gives climb milling: CW around an outside contour, CCW inside a pocket.
  • Start and cancel on a straight G00 / G01 move in the XY plane, longer than the tool radius, away from the part.
  • Inside corners smaller than the tool radius cause an overcut alarm. Avoid more than one block without XY motion while compensation is on.
  • Sinumerik: the D number comes with the tool (T1 D1). NORM / KONT choose the approach behaviour.
